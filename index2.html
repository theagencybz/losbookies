Training — how Los Bookies Hub works

Last update: 20 Oct 2026 — Register till splits Book Sales vs Other Sales (services). History has monthly GL drill-down, date filters, and Journal entry for bank reclasses / cash-to-bank. Multi-book pre-orders via “+ Another book”. Gift wrap is not a service category (use free gift wrap perk or Other service if charged).

The big picture

  1. Customer reserves a book → you take a deposit (liability, not a sale yet).
  2. You batch books and buy them from a vendor (Amazon, Book Depository, etc.).
  3. You match the bank credit card charges to those vendor orders.
  4. Books ship in a freight box to Belize; you receive and price stock.
  5. Customer picks up → convert deposit to a sale (or use POS).
  6. Every Friday / month-end → confirm till on Register, review History, post the same lines in QuickBooks.

Each tab (current layout)

  • Sales → Pre-Orders — Customer + book + deposit. Status starts Pending Purchase. Use + Another book to log multiple titles for the same customer. Select rows → Batch for a vendor order. Add Stock or Other lines in the batch modal.
  • Sales → Register — POS cart + Today’s till → QBO. Book sales and service (Other) sales are separate GLs. Confirm posting marks the day and saves a report under History.
  • Sales → AR — Open balances (pre-order remainders + credit sales). Receive payment (Cash / ABL / BBL). Invoice jumps to Register with deposit applied.
  • Sales → Inventory — Stock, services (non-inventory), write-offs, move Bookstore ↔ Library. Services: Shipping, Membership, Printing, Other — not gift wrap as a paid category.
  • Sales → History — Monthly GL summary (This month / 3 months / All / Custom). Click any amount to see the transactions. Journal entry for cash-to-bank and wrong-account fixes. Posting history lists till posts and journals.
  • Buying — Sub-tabs: Orders, CC Charges, Freight / Consolidation. Receive stock: landed cost, holds (no invoice yet), short-ships. Invoice only at pickup on Register.
  • OWL — Orange Walk Library staff desk (holds, loans, returns). Public library shelves open from the storefront CTA.
  • Training — This page + Load Full Demo.
  • Library (header) — Staff view of library-located titles.
  • Storefront — Customer shelves by age; only published stock.

Weekly posting schedule (QuickBooks)

Close the week on Friday. If month-end falls mid-week, also post on that day so the month is complete.

  • Customer deposits (pre-orders) — Sum of deposits taken that week.
    Dr Bank (Cash / ABL / BBL) · Cr Customer deposits (liability)
  • Book sales — Inventory items sold at Register (excl. GST split if you track it).
    Dr Bank · Cr Book Sales · Cr GST Payable
  • Other sales — Services (shipping, membership, printing, etc.).
    Dr Bank · Cr Other Sales · Cr GST Payable
  • Cash to bank — When you deposit the till drawer. Do the bank deposit in QBO; optionally log the same move here with History → Journal entry (Cash drawer → ABL/BBL).
  • Adding stock (freight received) — Landed cost of that box.
    Dr Inventory · Cr Inventory in-transit (or CC payable)
  • Credit collections — Payments on open AR.
    Dr Bank · Cr Accounts receivable
  • Corrections — Wrong bank, mis-tagged deposit: History → Journal entry, then mirror in QBO.

On Register, use Confirm posting to QB for the day’s till. On History, review the monthly grid and posting list so you don’t double-post.

Money types (don’t mix them up)

  • Deposit — Customer paid early for a pre-order. Liability until the book is delivered / invoiced.
  • Invoice / AR balance — Customer still owes the remainder (or a full credit sale).
  • Paid sale — Cash or transfer at POS; no open balance.
