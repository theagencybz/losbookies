Training — how Los Bookies Hub works

Last update: 26 Sep 2026 — Sales tab merges Pre-Orders · Register · AR · QBO. Invoice only after arrival. Deposit must pick a specific pre-order. Freight renamed Consolidation.

The big picture

  1. Customer reserves a book → you take a deposit (liability, not a sale yet).
  2. You batch books and buy them on Amazon.
  3. You match the bank credit card charges to those Amazon orders.
  4. Books ship in a freight box to Belize; you receive and price stock.
  5. Customer picks up → convert deposit to a sale (or use POS).
  6. Every Friday / month-end → post the week’s money in QBO Journal.

Each tab

  • Pre-Orders — Register customer + book + deposit only. Status starts as Pending Purchase. Select rows → Batch to build an Amazon order. You can also add Stock (no buyer) or Other lines (shipping, etc.).
  • Accounts Receivable — Who still owes money: open pre-order balances and credit sales. Receive Payment collects cash / transfer (ABL or BBL). Invoice jumps to POS with deposit pre-filled for pickup.
  • Buying — One place for Amazon work with three sub-tabs: Orders, CC Charges (import/log & link), Freight (boxes & receive). Credit-card / box icons on an order ask you which charge or box to use.
  • Receive stock — (1) Costs: unit cost, line total, prelanding cost, freight split equally by qty, landed/unit BZD. (2) Customer holds: pre-order copies are reserved — no invoice yet. (3) Short-ships. (4) Stock journal. Invoice only when the customer picks up (POS).
  • POS — Sales register. Invoice from Pre-Orders/AR opens POS with deposit applied; collect the balance then.
  • QBO Journal — Weekly checklist: deposits, sales, stock receive, AR collections. Pick a template, copy the lines into QuickBooks, then Confirm posting here.
  • Training — This page.
  • Storefront (header) — Customer-facing shelves by age. Only published stock appears.

Weekly posting schedule (QuickBooks)

Close the week on Friday. If month-end falls mid-week, also post on that day so the month is complete.

  • Customer deposits (pre-orders) — Sum of deposits taken that week.
    Dr Bank (Cash / ABL / BBL) · Cr Customer deposits (liability)
  • Cash & transfer sales — POS paid sales + pickups fully paid.
    Dr Bank · Cr Sales (incl. GST split if you track it)
  • Adding stock (when a freight box is received) — Landed cost of that box.
    Dr Inventory · Cr Inventory in-transit
  • Credit collections — Payments received on open AR.
    Dr Bank · Cr Accounts receivable

Use the QBO Journal tab to generate the lines, post them in QuickBooks, then hit Confirm posting here so you don’t double-post.

Money types (don’t mix them up)

  • Deposit — Customer paid early for a pre-order. Liability until the book is delivered / invoiced.
  • Invoice / AR balance — Customer still owes the remainder (or a full credit sale).
  • Paid sale — Cash or transfer at POS; no open balance.
